1. Project Overview

My project is AgroVision AI – Smart Crop Disease Detection, Yield Prediction & Market Intelligence Platform.

This project is built to help farmers make better decisions using Artificial Intelligence and real-time agricultural data.

Domain

Agriculture Tech + Artificial Intelligence

This project operates at the intersection of modern agriculture and advanced AI, aiming to bring accessible, data-driven solutions to traditional farming practices across rural areas.

Sub-domains

Computer Vision, Predictive Analytics, NLP

Leverages Computer Vision for image-based disease diagnosis, Predictive Analytics for weather-integrated yield forecasting, and NLP for the multilingual AI assistant.

ML Type

Supervised Learning, Deep Learning

Utilizes Supervised Learning with heavily labeled datasets (images and tabular data) and Deep Learning architectures capable of complex spatial and non-linear pattern recognition.

Algorithms

CNN, EfficientNet, Random Forest

CNN and EfficientNet are optimized for robust leaf image classification, while Random Forest handles high-dimensional, non-linear regression on environmental yield data.

Core Technologies

CNN EfficientNet OpenCV Random Forest FastAPI MongoDB React

2. Problem Statement

Major crop losses caused by delayed crop disease detection and unpredictable crop yields due to changing environmental conditions.

Why this problem is important?: Farmers suffer huge economic losses globally due to crop diseases and unpredictable environmental changes. Early detection and accurate forecasting are critical for global food security and sustainable farming.

How ML helps solve it?: Machine Learning enables real-time, automated analysis of crop leaf images to instantly identify diseases. Furthermore, it leverages historical and environmental data to predict crop yield, assisting farmers in making timely, data-driven decisions.

Disease Detection Delay

Farmers often notice crop diseases too late, leading to significant yield loss and excessive pesticide use.

Weather Uncertainty

Unpredictable climate conditions make it difficult to plan irrigation and harvesting optimally.

Market Fluctuation

Lack of real-time pricing data prevents farmers from selling at the best possible time and price.

Lack of Expert Guidance

Limited access to agricultural experts leaves farmers relying on guesswork rather than data.

How AI Solves This

AgroVision provides instant disease diagnosis via smartphone cameras, integrates live weather APIs for precision planning, and uses machine learning to forecast yields and track market trends, putting expert knowledge directly into the hands of farmers.

5. Data Analysis & Preprocessing

Image Processing (Disease)

  • Resize: Standardized to 224x224
  • Normalize: Pixel values scaled to [0, 1]
  • Augmentation: Rotation, flip, zoom to prevent overfitting
  • Split: 80% Train, 10% Val, 10% Test

Tabular Data (Yield)

  • Missing Values: Imputation using median/mode
  • Encoding: One-hot encoding for Soil/Crop types
  • Scaling: StandardScaler applied to numericals
  • Outliers: IQR method applied to clean anomalies

6. Machine Learning Methodology

CNN / EfficientNet

14. Key Technical Clarifications

Why use CNN for disease detection?
CNNs (Convolutional Neural Networks) are exceptionally proficient at spatial feature extraction. They can detect minute patterns in leaves (like spots or discoloration) that signify specific diseases, outperforming traditional ML models on image data.
Why Random Forest for yield prediction?
Yield data is tabular and involves non-linear relationships (e.g., rainfall vs. yield). Random Forest handles non-linearity well, requires less preprocessing than neural networks, and provides feature importance which helps understand what impacts yield most.
Why MongoDB?
MongoDB provides a flexible NoSQL schema, perfect for storing varied prediction histories, dynamic user profiles, and logs without rigid migrations during rapid prototyping.
Why is Telugu support included?
To ensure maximum accessibility for farmers in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, breaking the language barrier in agricultural tech.
How scalable is the architecture?
The React frontend (Vite) is statically hosted, and the FastAPI backend is stateless. This allows horizontal scaling via Docker containers or serverless deployments, capable of handling thousands of concurrent inferences.
